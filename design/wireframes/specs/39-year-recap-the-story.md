12. Your year in detail after the story
A scrolling page: top 50 lists, months and Show the math.

1. Length. 10 to 12 cards, about 75 seconds, never more than 90. Eight are fixed (intro, minutes, top song, top artist, top album, your sound, personality, closer) and 2 to 4 rotate. A card runs 6 to 8 s and a list card 9 s. More than 12 turns it into homework.

2. Arc. Opener, a build of the big numbers (minutes, song, artist, album), a turn (the sound drifted), surprises (the rotating beats), identity (the personality), then the close. The order follows these roles, so it feels built and not like a list of stats.

3. Rotating beats. A pool of about 12: listening clock, biggest discovery, comeback, obsession week, longest streak, song of each season, most replayed day, mood arc, most-sung lyric line and a household deep cut (opt in). Each is scored by how far it sits from this profile's own baseline, how personal it is and how different it is from beats already chosen. A beat needs enough data: a streak needs 7 days and a discovery needs 50 plays.

4. Top 5. Each fixed card shows number one big and reveals the rest of its top 5 after 2 s. Full lists live on the detail page.

5. Sound. Song cards play 12 s of the track, starting where the profile most often jumped to, otherwise 30% in, with a 400 ms crossfade. The Sound button in the top bar is remembered on each device. Offline and not downloaded: silent, with a small note. [HOLD] pauses the sound too.

6. Taps. [TAP] the right 65% goes forward and the left 35% goes back. [HOLD] pauses and hides the controls. [SWIPE] down closes, and it resumes where it left off for 30 days. [GUARD] the first tap is ignored for 300 ms after a card appears.

7. Copy. Sentences come from a bank of templates chosen by the seed. Numbers come only from queries. Friendly, never judgmental, no mood or health claims. Kid profiles skip the late-night beats.

8. Share. Every card exports on the device as a 9:16 and a 1:1 still. Nothing is uploaded and there is no watermark. An option hides song and artist names first.

9. Detail page. After the story: top 50 songs, artists and albums, month by month, and every number behind the personality. Show the math on any card lists the counts and the rule, such as "Deep Diver: your top six artists were 34% of plays".

10. Honest counting. The same song on two albums counts once. Listened time is counted, not track length. Private listening, Guest, interruptions and plays under 2 s are excluded. Every card says what date it is counted through.

11. Accessibility. Every card has a text version read in order, every chart has its values as text, nothing flashes more than three times a second, and auto-advance is off while a screen reader runs. Reduce Motion shows the still final frame.

12. Motion. Smooth and unhurried in every pack. Shapes ease in over 400 to 600 ms and numbers count up over 600 ms. Ambient motion (Tide's waves, Bloom's colour fields, Glass's glow) drifts at 8 to 14 s a cycle, is capped at 30 fps and stops in the background and in Low Power Mode. Editorial uses slow wipes and rolling numerals. Backgrounds are vector shapes, not video.
