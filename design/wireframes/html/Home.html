Home
1
Profile button. TAPopens the profile switcher sheet. HOLDquick list of signed-in profiles. GUARDlocked profiles ask for PIN or biometrics; switching while music plays asks "Keep playing?". HAPTICsuccess on switch.
2
Made for you tiles. TAPopens the playlist. HOLDmenu: Play, Shuffle, Pin, Why these songs, Regenerate. SWIPEscrolls and snaps one tile at a time. 2×TAPnothing, so nobody likes a playlist by accident. MOTIONtile presses to 0.97; Android's first tile is wide and shrinks as it scrolls off.
3
Recently played. TAPopens the album or playlist, never starts playback. HOLDitem menu. SWIPEscrolls. Items appear after 30 s of play, so a mis-tap leaves no trace.
4
Rediscover. Albums you loved and have not played in 60+ days. TAPopens. HOLDmenu with "Not interested", which removes it and trains the recommender.
5
Mini player. TAPbody expands Now Playing. TAPplay or next acts in place. SWIPEup expands. Left skips forward and right goes back, with the next song sliding in under the finger (board 42). GUARDtransport buttons debounce 200 ms. HAPTICsoft on play and next.
6
Tab bar. TAPswitches tab. Tapping the active tab scrolls to top, a second tap returns to the tab's root. iOS minimizes the bar while scrolling down and restores it on scroll up; Android hides it the same way. Search is its own glass button on iOS and the fourth destination on Android.
Whole screen. SWIPEpull down at 72 pt to refresh the library; haptic when armed. Content loads from the local mirror first, so the first frame is never empty.
States. New profile: one card, "Play something to train your mixes", plus library shortcuts. Fewer than 50 plays: up to three mixes. Building: skeleton tiles on iOS, expressive loading indicator on Android. Offline: only downloaded items stay active, the rest dim with a banner. Server unreachable: cached library stays usable with a dismissible banner. Unsynced changes: small dot on the avatar.
