Encore on the projector

A separate app, "Encore TV", built from the same code as the phone app: playback, queue, lyrics timing and your server are all shared. These frames show how it looks on a 16:9 projector and how the remote drives it. Songs, artists and lyrics are made up for the drawing.

The light ring is where the remote is. The projector has 1 GB of memory, so the TV app leaves out the moving colour background and keeps fewer pictures in memory. Sound analysis runs on your phone, not here.

The app

Sleep mode

Full screen and nearly black: the words are a dim warm grey, never white, so the room stays dark. Encore also asks the screen to turn its brightness down; some projectors ignore that, which is why the picture itself is kept dark. The screen stays awake while music plays and is free to sleep once it stops.

The remote

  • ArrowsMove the light ring between things
  • OKOpen or play what has the ring
  • Hold OKA song's menu: Play next, Add to playlist and the rest
  • BackUp one level; leaves Sleep mode
  • Play/Pause, skipWork from anywhere in the app
  • Menu keyAlso opens a song's menu, on remotes that have it

Signing in on the projector uses Jellyfin's Quick Connect: the projector shows a short code, and you approve it on your phone, so nothing is typed with the remote. If the app ever crashes, the next launch shows a few lines about what went wrong, to read out or photograph, since the projector can't be reached for logs.
