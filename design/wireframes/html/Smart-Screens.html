Notes
1
Why this song. Opened from More on Now Playing, a queue row or a mix tile. It shows one or two reasons, never more, and up to two "sounds like" songs that open on tap. More like this counts as +1.5 for the song and its neighbours in this context but does not add a like. Fewer like this counts as −1.0. Both give a light haptic and a one-line confirmation.
2
Not interested. Scope is this song or this artist. Reasons change what is learned: Not my taste lowers the song and its neighbours, Heard it too much only rests the song for 30 days, Wrong moment lowers it in that time of day only. The song is removed from every smart surface at once, while the user can still play it by choice. Confirming gives a warning haptic. The same sheet opens from an artist page.
3
Adventurousness. Three steps: Familiar 5%, Balanced 10%, Adventurous 20% of slots. It applies from the next refill and never reshuffles a list the user is listening to.
4
Learn from my listening. Turning it off asks once, keeps saved history, and switches smart surfaces to the plain fallbacks. Turning it on again does not back-fill the time it was off.
5
Reset suggestions. A confirm sheet with a red Reset button, separated from Cancel. It forgets the learned model, co-listen graph and mixes and starts a new learning epoch. Likes, playlists, history and the Not interested list stay. Not interested has its own Clear list button.
6
Clear listening history. Deletes the events on every device and the server. No device can bring them back. The year-end recap then starts from the day it was cleared.
7
How suggestions are doing. A private, local page. It shows skip rate of app-chosen songs, completion, distinct artists per 20 songs and repeats for each shelf over the last 4 weeks, and nothing leaves the device.
8
Mix card states. Every state above has a text label, so colour is never the only signal. A mix never shows an empty tile, and a failed build keeps the last good mix with its age.
