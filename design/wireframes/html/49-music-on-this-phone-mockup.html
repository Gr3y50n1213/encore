Encore · mock-up 49 · Phase 12 stage 4

Music on this phone

Songs stored on the phone become a source next to your server, in the same profile. They show up in Library, Search, mixes and the car like any other song, and they play without internet. Only Settings and the first-run Connect screen change. Android is shown. iOS follows the same steps in its own style.

Behaviour

  1. One profile, two sources. The phone's music joins the open profile next to its server, so history, likes, playlists, mixes and the companion server's sync work across both.
  2. The same song in both places. It appears once, matched by its MusicBrainz ID, or else by artist, title and length. It plays from the phone's copy, which needs no internet and uses no data.
  3. What is read. Title, artist, album, album artist, track and disc number, year, genre, length, the embedded cover (or a cover.jpg or folder.jpg beside the files), and lyrics from the file's tags or a matching .lrc file. MP3, FLAC, M4A (AAC and ALAC), Ogg Vorbis, Opus and WAV.
  4. Likes and playlists. A like on a song that is only on the phone stays in Encore (and syncs through the companion server). Such a song can be added to an Encore playlist, but not to a server playlist, because the server has no copy.
  5. Files that change. A deleted or moved file drops out of the library on the next look. Its plays stay in your history. A folder on an SD card that is taken out shows "Not available right now" and comes back when the card returns.
  6. Speed. The first look of about 1,000 songs takes a minute or two on a mid-range phone and runs in the background. Covers are kept small, the same way server covers are cached.

To approve

  • How Encore gets to the files. Recommended: you pick folders in Android's folder picker (screen 2). Encore sees only those folders and their .lrc lyric files, and Android asks no extra permission. The other way is Android's "Music and audio" permission, which lets Encore find every song on the phone with no folders to pick, but it also sees voice notes and recordings, and Encore can't read .lrc lyric files that way.
  • The same song in both places plays from the phone. The other way plays it from the server and only uses the phone's copy when offline.
  • "Use music on this phone" on the Connect screen, for people with no server.
  • The phone mark on albums that are only on the phone (screen 4).
