What I need from you

  • Frame for Mixes and Recently played: A (the lead item's album color, softened) or B (neutral, follows your wallpaper). Both are drawn above.
  • Anything off in the look: type size, how round the shapes are, how bold the color is. Send it as you see it and I'll change the mock-up before any code.

Real widgets use Android's system font and a gradient drawn from the same math as the app's Now Playing background. This page approximates that with CSS, so edges and blends will differ a little on your phone. The sizes use Google's cell table; Samsung's launcher may differ slightly.
