Shuffle, Repeat, Autoplay. TAPtoggles. Repeat cycles off, all, one, with a "1" badge. Shuffle reorders Up Next only, never History. HOLDAutoplay opens the Familiar to Adventurous slider. HAPTICtoggle on and off; on Android the pressed button morphs between round and square.

History. Collapsed by default. TAP"Show 12" expands, newest at the bottom. Rows are dimmed; TAPa row plays it again and pushes the current song into History.
3
Now playing row. Highlighted, with live level bars. TAPreturns to the player. SWIPEnone, so the current song cannot be dismissed by a slip.
4
Up Next rows. TAPjumps to the song; skipped songs move to History and a toast offers Undo. HOLDthe handle lifts the row to reorder, with a haptic on pick up and drop. SWIPEright: Play Next. Left, past 60%: Remove, with Undo for 5 s; the haptic fires when the action arms. GUARDthe row body ignores drags, only the handle reorders, so scrolling never moves a song.
5
Autoplay rows. The same, plus swipe left on the red "Not this" action, which removes the song and counts as a strong dislike. HOLDmenu includes "Why this song". Reordering an autoplay row moves it into Up Next.
6
Clear. TAPopens a confirm sheet with a separated destructive "Clear Up Next". Autoplay is not cleared; it refills from the current song.
States. Empty Up Next: "Nothing queued. Autoplay continues with songs you'll like." Autoplay off: the section is one row, "Autoplay is off", with a toggle. Refilling: three skeleton rows. Offline: songs that are not downloaded dim and are skipped automatically. Songs added from anywhere show a toast, "Added to Up Next", with Undo.
