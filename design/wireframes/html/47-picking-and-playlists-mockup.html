Picking songs, adding in one tap, and a playlist menu

Mock-up 47, 24 November 2026. You found the picking screen plain and adding to a playlist still too many steps. This version uses more of Material 3 Expressive: a coloured bar with a big count, picked songs joined into one group, a toolbar with a big Add button, a one-tap "Add to" your last playlist everywhere, and a press-and-hold menu on playlists.
