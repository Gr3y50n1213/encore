Search
1
Search field. iOS puts it at the bottom in glass, within thumb reach; Android docks it at the top. TAPopens the keyboard and shows results as you type, matched against the on-device library index in under 50 ms, typo tolerant, lyrics included when the server has them. The return key saves the query. SWIPEdown on results dismisses the keyboard. Android's scope chips: All, Library, Downloaded.
2
Recent searches. Saved per profile and never synced to anyone else. TAPfills the field and runs it. SWIPEleft or tap Remove deletes one. HOLDon the header offers "Clear all" in a confirm sheet.
3
Browse by mood. Tiles come from the audio analysis, not genre tags. TAPopens a mix for that mood. Android's tiles alternate two leaf shapes, round at one pair of corners and tight at the other, so the grid reads as a pattern. Tiles are plain color, never artwork.
Results while typing. Sections: Top Result, Songs, Albums, Artists, Playlists. TAPsong plays it and starts autoplay from it. TAPother types open them. HOLDitem menu. GUARDthe list is frozen from touch-down until release, so a row that re-ranks under the finger never launches the wrong song.
States. Idle (shown). Typing, results (above). No match: "Nothing matches [query]" with a tip to check spelling; if an online lyrics provider is on, "Search lyrics online" appears. Empty library: "Connect a server to search". Offline: search still works from the local index, and songs that are not downloaded dim.
