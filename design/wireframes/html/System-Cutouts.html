Notch, Dynamic Island, cutouts and foldables

Every screen has to work around a camera cut-out, a gesture area and, on foldables, a fold. The rule is the same everywhere: backgrounds run edge to edge, controls stay inside the safe area, and nothing tappable ever crosses a fold. iPhone Duo and the new MacBook Pro are announced Apple products. Items marked Reported come from press coverage or developer write-ups and should be checked against Apple's and Samsung's own documentation.

Rules
1
Read the insets from the system. iOS: the safe area. Android: the window insets for system bars, display cutout and gestures. Mac: NSScreen.safeAreaInsets with the left and right areas beside the notch. Flutter on Windows and Linux: the view padding. Never type numbers like 20, 44 or 59.
2
Draw behind, place inside. Album gradients and artwork run edge to edge under the status bar, island, notch and gesture area. Text, buttons, sliders and drag handles sit inside the safe area.
3
Status text follows the artwork. The clock and battery turn white on dark gradients and dark on light covers, with a 200 ms cross-fade when the album changes.
4
Gesture zones. Keep sliders and swipe areas at least 24 dp from the screen edges. A touch that starts in the edge zone belongs to the system back gesture, not to a scrub.
5
Mac menu bar item. One optional item. If the bar is crowded macOS can hide items behind the notch, so everything it does is also in the Dock menu, the mini player and the keyboard shortcuts.
6
Live Activities on Mac. An iPhone Live Activity already mirrors to the Mac menu bar. Whether a Mac app can start its own is not documented yet Reported, so Encore plans for the mirror only.
7
Touch on Mac. On Macs with a touch screen, controls use gesture recognizers only, targets grow to 36 pt, and every hover-only detail has a touch route. Test on a touch Mac when one is available; until then, review every hover-only detail by hand.
8
Folding keeps the session. The Rust core owns playback, queue and position, and screen state is saved outside the view, so a fold, unfold, rotate or window resize never restarts the song or loses the scroll position. Orientation is never locked. Android 16 ignores orientation and resize opt-outs on large screens for apps that target it.
9
Keep off the fold. Nothing tappable, no slider thumb and no line of text crosses the fold or hinge gap. Android reads it from the folding feature (state, orientation, separating, bounds, occlusion). On iPhone Duo the equivalent is a reserved region at the fold plus an arrangement for split or overlay layouts Reported; those API names come from a developer write-up of Apple’s Tech Talks, so confirm them in the Xcode documentation.
10
Postures. Half open with a horizontal hinge: content on top, controls below. Half open with a vertical hinge or flat and wide: two panes. Closed: compact. Show a short transition when entering or leaving tabletop, as Samsung asks for Flex mode.
11
Cover screens. A Fold cover screen runs the compact layout. A Flip cover screen shows the system player and widgets; running Encore there is optional and needs the full-screen, any-aspect-ratio and state-keeping rules Samsung lists.
12
Test matrix before release. iPhone with island and notch, iPhone with a home button, Mac with notch and with island and touch, Pixel, Samsung centre and corner hole, a Fold and a Flip, Pixel Fold, iPhone Duo, an Android tablet, landscape, split screen, 200% font and right to left.
