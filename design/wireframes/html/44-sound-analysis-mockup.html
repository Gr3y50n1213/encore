Sound analysis, as people will meet it

New places in Encore, drawn with demo songs and numbers. Nothing here is built yet: this page is for deciding what each screen says and does before any code is written.

Proposed order (23 November 2026, features first): build 2, 6 and 4 now: the Settings page and its stop messages are what turn analysis on and show it working, and "Sounds like" uses data the core already sends. Build 1, 3 and 5 after the mood engine exists in the core, since mood tiles and mood mixes need it.

Settled underneath, so you don't have to choose: it is opt-in, runs on Wi-Fi while charging by default, reads about a megabyte per song without making your server transcode, and keeps about 1,600 small numbers per song. The numbers on these screens (8,106 songs, 9 GB, 5 hours) are examples; the real ones come from your library and your phone's own measured speed.
