Output and handoff
1
Continue banner. Appears when this app opens while another of your devices is playing. TAPContinue pulls playback here. SWIPEup dismisses it, and it also fades after 8 s. GUARDit never transfers by itself, never shows while this device is playing, and shows once per session for each device.
2
Sheet. Opened from Output on Now Playing, the mini player, or the banner. SWIPEdown closes. Detents: medium and full.
3
This device. Selected row with level bars while sound plays here. TAPpulls playback back from a remote device.
4
System outputs. iOS embeds the system route picker for AirPlay and Bluetooth. Android opens the system media output switcher. The app cannot see which speaker was chosen, only that the route changed.
5
Your devices. Only devices signed in to the same profile. TAPmoves playback there: snapshot of queue, position, shuffle, repeat and autoplay context goes over the sync connection, the target preloads and confirms, then this device fades out. HAPTICsuccess when confirmed, error if it fails, in which case this device keeps playing and a toast says why. HOLDmenu: Control only (keep sound here), Rename, Sign out this device. Shared household devices need a confirm first.
6
Offline devices. Dimmed. TAPshakes with the warning haptic and the reason. They disappear after 30 days.
Remote control. While another device plays, Now Playing shows a capsule, "Playing on Living Room Mac". Transport, scrub, queue edits and volume act on that device. Tap the capsule to move playback here.
States. No other devices: "Sign in on another device to hand off music." Companion server unreachable: the devices section is disabled with the reason and a retry. Different profile on the other device: not shown.
