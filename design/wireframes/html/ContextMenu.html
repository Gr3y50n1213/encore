Song menu
1
Opening. HOLD400 ms on Android (it follows the system setting) or the system menu timing on iOS, on any song row or tile. The row lifts to 1.03 with a shadow, everything else dims and blurs. iOS menu morphs out of the row as one glass shape; Android's popup scales from the row. HAPTICfires at 400 ms, LONG_PRESS on Android. GUARDmoving more than 10 pt before 400 ms cancels it, so scrolling never opens a menu.
2
Queue group. Play Next, Add to Queue, Add to Playlist. Queue actions close the menu and show a toast with Undo. Add to Playlist opens a sheet where several playlists can be ticked at once, plus a New Playlist row.
3
Library group. Like toggles (the label becomes Unlike), Download becomes Remove Download when saved, Share sends a link to the song's metadata, never the file.
4
Navigate group. Go to Album and Go to Artist are hidden on pages that already are that album or artist. Why this song appears only for songs the app picked.
5
Destructive group. Separated by a gap and red text, always last. Not interested hides the song from suggestions and trains the recommender, with Undo for 5 s. Remove from Library needs a confirm sheet whose destructive button sits apart from Cancel.
Choosing. TAPan item. GUARDlifting the finger without sliding picks nothing, and the first item is never auto-selected. SWIPEor tap outside closes with no change. Items past the screen edge scroll inside the menu.
States. Offline: Download and Share dim with a reason. Song unavailable: only Remove and Go to Album remain. Already in the queue: Add to Queue shows a small check and adds a second copy only on a deliberate second tap. Multi-select (Library, Playlist): the same menu applies to every selected song, and counts appear in the labels.
