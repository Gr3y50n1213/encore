Transport. TAPprevious restarts after 3 s, otherwise goes back. HOLDprevious or next scans at 4× with the haptic ticking each second. GUARD200 ms debounce. On Android the three buttons are separate rounded squares 8 dp apart: Previous and Next are tonal and shaped by their arrow: the end the arrow points to is fully round (36 dp) and the end beside play is 10 dp, so Previous is round on the left and Next on the right. Play is wider and changes shape with its state. Playing it is a rounded square (the flatter shape, 24 dp), paused it morphs to a full round shape, and pressed it tightens to 14 dp (see the Buttons board).
