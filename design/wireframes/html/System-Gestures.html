Gestures and accidental presses

One gesture vocabulary on both platforms so a screen never surprises anyone. Anything that changes playback, deletes, or leaves the screen is either undoable for 5 seconds or needs a deliberate second action.

Gesture vocabulary

GestureTiming and distanceMeans
TapRelease within 300 ms, under 10 pt of driftPrimary action: play, open, toggle
Double tapTwo taps within 250 ms, same spotOnly on artwork: like. Never on rows or buttons.
Long pressAndroid 400 ms (follows the system setting). iOS the system menu timing, about 0.5 sContext menu for that item. Haptic when it opens, menu opens with the item lifted.
Horizontal swipe on a rowPast 25% of row width arms, past 60% commitsLeading: Play Next. Trailing: Add to Queue, Remove.
Vertical swipe on a playerVelocity and distance, follows the fingerDown: collapse. Up on mini player: expand. Sideways on the mini player or artwork: skip, board 42.
Drag handleStarts on touch of the handle onlyReorder queue and playlists. With a mouse or trackpad the whole row drags, board 43.
ScrubDrag the progress bar, vertical drift slows itSeek. Drift down to 50% or 25% speed for fine scrub.
Edge swipe from leftStarts within 24 pt of the edgeBack. Predictive on Android.
Pull downArmed at 72 ptRefresh library from the server

Accidental-press protection

TargetsEvery control is at least 44 pt on iOS and 48 dp on Android. Icon buttons that sit close together keep 8 pt between hit areas.
Cancel by sliding offTouch down highlights, the action runs on release inside the control. Sliding out cancels with no haptic.
Transport debouncePlay, pause, next and previous ignore a second tap for 200 ms after a first, so a bouncing thumb does not skip two songs.
Skip is reversibleA skip pushes the song onto History. Previous within 3 s of a new track returns to the one just skipped, never two back.
Destructive actionsRemove from library, delete playlist, sign out and clear downloads need a confirm sheet with the destructive button separated from Cancel. Remove from queue and remove from a playlist show an Undo toast for 5 s instead.
Pocket and bagThe lock screen controls and headphone buttons keep working. In-app, a touch that lands during the first 150 ms after the screen wakes is ignored.
Scrub lockWhile the finger is on the progress bar, edge-back, collapse-swipe and tab taps are ignored so a slip never closes the player.
MenusA long-press menu opens under the finger. Releasing without moving never triggers an item; the first item needs a second tap.
Blocked actionsA tap that cannot work (offline, not downloaded) shakes the control 4 pt, fires the warning haptic, and shows a one-line reason.
Unsent changesLikes and playlist edits apply instantly on the device and sync later. A failed sync shows a small badge, never a blocking error.
