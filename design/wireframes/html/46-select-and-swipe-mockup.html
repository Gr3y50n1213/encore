Pick many songs at once, and a softer swipe

Mock-up 46, 24 November 2026. Adding songs to a playlist one at a time was slow. Here you pick as many songs as you like and add them all in one go, anywhere songs are listed. Swiping a song row now opens a round bubble that stretches into a pill, like Gmail.

2. The swipe

What stays the same

  • Long press on a song still opens its menu, and on the projector holding OK does too. Picking songs on the projector: "Select songs" from the ⋮ menu, then OK on each song.
  • The swipe directions don't change: right is Play next, left is Add to queue, or Remove where that's the row's job.
  • With Reduce motion on, the bubble appears at its full size with no stretch and no pop.
