1
Header. TAPreturns to the artwork view. Art and title stay pinned so the song is always identifiable.
2
Past lines. 40% opacity and a 1.2 px blur, so the eye stays on the present. They keep their position until the list scrolls.
3
Current line. Full brightness. Each word fills left to right across its own sung time with a soft light edge; lines with only line timing light up whole. The active line sits about 30% from the top. MOTIONevery line moves on a spring, delayed 18 ms per line of distance up to six lines, so the column ripples. Reduce Motion: cross-fade only.
4
Second singer. Duet lines align right, same size, so who is singing reads at a glance.
5
Background vocals. Smaller and dimmer, shown under the main line while sung.
6
Instrumental gap. For gaps over 5 s, three dots breathe in time and fill one by one before the next line. They collapse the moment singing starts.
7
Upcoming lines. TAPany line seeks to its start and shows a 4 s pill, "Back to 2:31", that undoes the jump. GUARDa touch that moved more than 10 pt is a scroll, never a seek. HOLDselects lines for sharing, capped at five lines per image card.
8
Controls. Same as Now Playing, compact. After 4 s with no touch they fade and lyrics fill the screen; a tap on empty space brings them back. SWIPEby hand pauses auto-scroll until 3 s after release.
States. Synced word by word, synced by line, and unsynced (plain scroll, no highlight, a small "Not synced" tag). Loading: five shimmer lines. No lyrics: "No lyrics for this song", with a search button only if an online provider is on. Instrumental flag: dots and the title only. Translation and pronunciation lines, when added, sit under each line at 60% size.
