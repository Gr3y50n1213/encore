The first release of Encore: a music player for the music you already own, on your own media server or on your phone. Nothing is sent to the project or to anyone else.

## What's here

- **Encore for Android** (`encore-1.0.0.apk`), for phones and tablets on Android 9 or later.
- **Encore TV** (`encore-tv-1.0.0.apk`), for Android TV, Google TV and Android projectors.
- **The companion server** (optional), for Windows, macOS, Linux and Docker (`ghcr.io/gr3y50n1213/encore-server:1.0.0`).

The Apple, Windows and Linux apps are not out yet. Their shared core is built and tested for every one of those platforms in CI.

## Where your music can come from

- Jellyfin.
- Emby.
- Navidrome and other OpenSubsonic servers.
- Plex.
- Folders on the phone itself. Encore reads only the folders you pick and never changes a file.

A song that is both on your server and on the phone is listed once, and plays from the phone.

## Highlights

- Library, search, album and artist pages, playlists you can edit, likes.
- Word-by-word synced lyrics, and Sleep mode with big lyrics.
- Mixes and autoplay that learn on your own devices, with "Why this song?", Not interested, Private listening and Guest mode.
- Profiles, each with its own history, and an optional PIN.
- Downloads and offline mode.
- Android Auto.
- Home-screen widgets, a Quick Settings tile, and a sleep timer that fades out.
- With the companion server: history, likes and mixes shared between your devices, and "Play on" to move music from one device to another.

## Install

1. Download `encore-1.0.0.apk` on the phone and open it. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser or file app.
2. Check the file if you like: its SHA-256 is in `SHA256SUMS`.
3. The companion server: see `INSTALL.md` in the server download, or `docs/server/INSTALL.md` in the repository.

The APKs are signed with the project's own key, so later releases install over this one.

## Privacy and security

No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no account with the project. Listening history stays on your devices and your own companion server. The full statement is `PRIVACY.md`. Report a security problem privately: see `SECURITY.md`.
