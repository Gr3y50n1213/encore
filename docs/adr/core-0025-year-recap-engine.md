# ADR core-0025: The year recap engine

Date: 27 November 2026
Status: accepted (Phase 11, the engine only; the design is the approved plan, sections 10 and 25, and boards 39 to 41)

## Context
Each profile gets a year recap that must come out byte-identical on the companion server and on every device (x86_64, aarch64, 32-bit ARM, Windows, Apple), keep counting until the year really ends, and look different every year. Plan section 25 fixes the counting rules, the thresholds, the story's shape and the look's rules, and calls every number a starting value. This ADR records how `core/encore-recap` meets that and every value it had to choose. The core API, the store, the server and the apps are wired up afterwards; nothing here touches them.

## Decision
1. **A pure function.** `build_recap(&RecapInput) -> RecapData`, and `build_recap_json(&str) -> String` for bindings. The input is plain serde data: the play log (this year and any earlier plays the caller has), the year, the profile ID, the kid and Recap-off flags, the offered packs, earlier recaps from the archive, album palettes, album track counts, song fingerprints, artists heard before, the hemisphere, the counted-through instant and the clear and join days. No I/O, no clock, no locale. Dependencies: serde, serde_json, sha2 and thiserror, all already in the workspace. `encore-sources` is not a dependency (it pulls in HTTP and file readers); the two small song-key pieces needed (splitting a `t1` key, the 2 s tolerance) are repeated in `songs.rs`, and its test pins the same frozen key as `encore-sources`' test, so a change shows up on both sides.
2. **Determinism.** No floating point anywhere: counts are integers, shares are permille rounded half up in `u128`, hues are whole degrees from integer RGB arithmetic. Every map is a `BTreeMap`; every sort and every "most" has a total tie-break (plays, then listened time, then key bytes). The plays are sorted into one canonical order over every field before anything is counted, so input order never matters. The only randomness is the seed, and every choice made from it is SHA-256 of the seed and a label, taken modulo the number of choices. Output structs serialize in declaration order with `serde_json::to_string`; `serde_json::Value` is never used on the output path, so the workspace's serde_json features cannot change the bytes. Names are cut by characters, not bytes, and no `to_lowercase` (whose Unicode tables change between Rust releases) is used.
3. **Counting** (section 25). Listened milliseconds, not track length. Not counted: plays under 2 s, end reasons `handedOff` (the other device's play carries the time on), `interrupted` (the plan lists interruptions as excluded) and `error`, offsets beyond ±18 h, plays that started after the counted-through instant, plays on local days before a clear, and explicit plays on kid profiles. A play lands on the calendar day of its own stored offset. Songs are grouped by key: `t1` keys with the same hash are walked by seconds, a group starts at its shortest key and takes every key within 2 s of that anchor, so every pair inside a group is "the same song" and grouping does not depend on order; the group shows the title, artist and album seen most and keeps the raw key played most (for the playlist). `mb:` keys stand alone.
4. **Kinds.** Under 10 plays: `none` (`noneReason: notEnoughPlays`). From 20 hours and 100 plays: `full`. In between: `short`, exactly three cards (minutes, top song, top artist) with the gentle line ("A short year so far. Your top song was ..."). Recap off: `none` (`off`), with no counting at all and the hash of an empty log.
5. **The story.** Eight fixed beats (intro, minutes, top song, top artist, top album, your sound, personality, closer) when their data exists, and 2 to 4 rotating beats from the pool: listening clock, biggest discovery, comeback, obsession week, longest streak, song of each season, most replayed day, mood arc and most-sung line. The household deep cut is not built (it needs other profiles and their consent); a TODO marks it in `facts.rs` and it is never emitted. Each rotating candidate has a distance from the profile's own baseline (0 to 1000, defined per beat in `facts.rs`) and a personalness (fixed per beat). Score = (6 × distance + 4 × personal) / 10 + a seed jitter of 0 to 40 − 300 for each theme (time, artist, song, sound, habit, new, voice) shared with a rotating beat already chosen. Greedy: the best is taken until four (or twelve cards) are reached; after two, a beat must score 250. Ties go to the beat id that sorts first. Beats are ordered by arc role (opener, big numbers, turn, surprises, identity, close) and a fixed order inside each role. A card runs 7 s, a list card (top song, top artist, your sound, seasons) 9 s, the intro 6 s, personality and closer 8 s; if the sum passes 90 s the latest non-list cards shorten to 6 s.
6. **The personality.** Twelve archetypes, each one metric against one threshold; the one that clears its threshold by the widest margin relative to the threshold wins, ties by table order, and the All-Rounder (shown with the top-six share) when none clears. Show the math gives the metric, the rule and every known metric. Kid profiles never get the Night Owl and have no late-night metric at all.
7. **Pack, seed, palette, layout.** The pack is chosen by rendezvous hashing: of the offered packs (one per id, highest version), not last year's, nor the year before's once six or more are offered, the one with the lowest SHA-256 of ("encore-recap/pack/v1", profile ID, year, pack id). It ignores list order and moves only when a new pack wins. If the rule leaves nothing it is relaxed, the year-before rule first. The seed is SHA-256 of ("encore-recap/seed/v1", profile ID, year, chosen pack id, pack version), first 8 bytes, so a pack that comes round again with a new version also gets new layouts (the plan's "seed of profile ID + year + pack version"; the pack itself cannot depend on its own version, hence the split). The palette takes the given colours of the top three albums that have one, in album and cluster order, de-duplicated, at most six; the lead is the first colour with chroma (max − min channel) of 40 or more whose hue is at least 60° from last year's lead, else the chromatic colour furthest from it (the "next best cluster"). With no album colours at all (art not cached), four colours are made from a seed hue 62° to 298° on from last year's. Fitting the palette to each pack's ground and the 4.5:1 contrast check stay with the renderers. Layout index and a shape seed per beat come from the seed; intro, minutes, top song, top artist, top album and personality have four layouts, the rest three.
8. **Words.** All sentences are in `words.rs`, two variants each, chosen by the seed; numbers are formatted by hand ("41,208", "11 pm", "Mar 9"). The year line takes the two strongest of eleven traits (strength = metric ÷ its threshold): late nights (not for kids), early mornings, loud choruses and soft edges (only with fingerprints on half the plays), whole albums, new names, songs on repeat, music every day, weekends, the top genre, a few favourites. English only. The mood arc speaks of the music's energy, never of the listener's mood.
9. **The document.** camelCase JSON: `schema` 1, `engine` 1, `year`, `kind`, `noneReason`, `soFar` (built before 31 December has ended in UTC−12, i.e. before 1 January 12:00 UTC), `locked` (built on or after 8 January 00:00 UTC), `countedThroughUtcMs`, `since` (a clear or join day inside the year; the later wins), `pack` and its version, `seed` (16 hex digits), `palette`, `yearLine`, `totals`, `beats[]` (each with role, seconds, layout, shape seed, score for rotating beats, template id, text, data and math), `detail` (top 50 songs, artists and albums, months, every personality metric; full story only) and `logHash`: SHA-256 over every play used (earlier years first, each in canonical order, fields joined by 0x1F and ended by 0x1E, numbers in decimal). Names over 100 characters are cut with an ellipsis. If the document would pass 100 KB the detail lists shrink to 25, 10, then none.
10. **Rollups.** `daily_rollups(&[Play]) -> Vec<DailyRollup>`: per local day, the total, per raw song key, per artist key and per hour, by the same counting rules. Raw keys, so a rollup never changes when a new key of the same song appears; the recap merges at build time. They are a pure function of the log, so they can be rebuilt at any time; the engine itself counts from the plays.

## Starting values chosen here (none measured)
| What | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Excluded end reasons | handedOff, interrupted, error (inProgress counts with the time so far) |
| Same-song grouping | anchor at the shortest key, members within 2 s of the anchor |
| Late night / morning / afternoon / evening | 22:00-04:59 / 05:00-11:59 / 12:00-16:59 / 17:00-21:59 local |
| Minutes "Most of it ..." sentence | that part of the day has 35% of listened time or more |
| Change against last year | within ±2%: "about the same" |
| Discovery | artist absent from every earlier play and `artistsBefore`; needs 50 plays (token); needs some baseline at all |
| Comeback | 20 plays before, silent 180 days or more, 20 plays this year |
| Obsession week | Monday-to-Sunday, 25 plays of one artist and 40% of that week |
| Streak | 7 days (token) |
| Song of each season | meteorological seasons by hemisphere; 10 plays a season, 3 such seasons, 2 different songs |
| Most replayed day | 5 plays of one song in one local day |
| Mood arc | half the plays fingerprinted, 6 months with 10 fingerprinted plays; a half-year change under 50 permille reads as steady |
| Most-sung line | 3 jumps; the top song's snippet starts at its most-jumped line when there is one |
| Your sound | genre on half the plays; a drift is a top-four genre whose average monthly share rises 8 points after a split month, two months each side |
| Top album start to finish | an unbroken run of plays of the album, none skipped, covering as many songs as the album has (needs `albumTrackCounts`) |
| Archetype thresholds | top six artists 30%, new artists 25%, from an album 60%, top artist 15%, top ten songs 20%, after 10 pm 35%, 5-10 am 30%, weekend 45%, 8 genres of 3%, music on 80% of days, radio or mix 40%, early skips 25% |
| A share counts | only when the field is known for half the plays |
| Scoring | weights 0.6 distance, 0.4 personal; jitter 0-40; shared theme −300; floor 250 after two |
| Personalness | most-sung line 900, comeback 850, discovery and obsession 800, replayed day 700, seasons 650, clock and mood arc 600, streak 500 |
| Card seconds | 6 intro, 7 card, 8 personality and closer, 9 list card; 90 s cap |
| Layouts | 4 for intro, minutes, top song, top artist, top album, personality; 3 for the rest |
| Palette | at most 6 colours; greys under chroma 40 never lead |
| Names | cut at 100 characters |
| Playlist | top 100 songs |

Values taken from `design/tokens.json` (`recap`): 10 to 12 cards, 8 fixed, 2 to 4 rotating, 90 s, list card 9 s, card 6 to 8 s, 100 KB, streak 7 days, discovery 50 plays, under 10 plays none, 20 hours and 100 plays full, 2 s minimum, 2 s same-song tolerance, top 3 albums for the palette, 60° hue gap, 3 to 4 layouts, last two packs avoided from 6.

## Unverified
- Every value in the table above, and the plan's own starting values (card timing, the 60° gap, the 20 hour and 100 play thresholds).
- Whether the stories read well: they have only been built from invented histories. The score weights and archetype thresholds need tuning on real years, with the owner's consent.
- The time for 100,000 plays was measured only on the development PC (see Testing), not on a mid-range phone.
- Cross-platform byte identity is argued from the design (no floats, ordered maps, SHA-256) and proven only on x86_64 Windows so far; the golden files are there for each platform's CI to prove it.
- `interrupted` plays are excluded on the plan's wording ("interruptions ... are excluded"); this drops real listening before a headphone unplug and may deserve a second look.
- The "joined mid-year" day needs a profile creation day from the caller (`joinedDay`); the engine never guesses it from the first play.
- Seasons follow meteorological months; the caller must pass the hemisphere.

## Testing
- Golden files (`core/encore-recap/tests/golden/`): very new (none), light (short), heavy full year with last year's recap, kid, cleared on 3 August, no fingerprints, a December "so far" build, and one profile in 2026 and 2027 (2027 is given 2026's pack and lead hue from its golden output: a different pack, hues at least 60° apart). `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1 cargo test -p encore-recap --test golden` regenerates them.
- Property tests over 60 generated profiles each: two builds, a shuffled log and reversed pack list, and the JSON entry point give the same bytes; card count, seconds and layouts stay in bounds; the next year never repeats the pack. Rollups are order-free and add up.
- 100,000 plays over 5,000 artists with 300-character names stay under 100 KB; an ignored timing test prints the build time.
- Unit tests: calendar arithmetic, local days across offsets, song-key splitting and grouping, hue maths, pack rules, palette gap, number formatting.
